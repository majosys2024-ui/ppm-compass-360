v1.9 | Faster Everyday Work, Smarter Status Reports & Present Mode

Latest Release
16 November 2026

Updating to 1.9

A Site Owner runs Setup once after updating (see upgrade instructions). Until then the app works as before and the new fields stay hidden. Existing licence keys keep working unchanged.

Data Kept: Setup only adds what is missing. All projects, reports, milestones, configurations and permissions stay as they are.

Highlights

  • 🗂️ Forms open on the right: milestones, risks and issues, change requests and decisions open in a panel over the page. If you have unsaved input, closing it asks Save / Don't save / Keep editing.
  • ↩️ Back and Forward work: every screen and tab has its own address, so the browser's Back button, Refresh and a copied link all bring you to the same place.
  • 🔔 Needs you: My Projects lists what is waiting for you, by your role: reports due, overdue milestones, High risks without an owner, change requests to decide and risks flagged for the sponsor.
  • 💡 Suggested ratings (optional): the status report suggests each rating and gives the reason. In its full mode, a rating better than the suggestion needs a reason, and All Projects marks those projects.
  • 💹 Estimate at completion: entered in the status report and shown on the Financials tab, in My Portfolio's Financial Burn (per project and for the portfolio) and in the exports.
  • ▶ Present: show a project, a portfolio or the Executive story as full-screen slides in a meeting, straight from the app.
  • 📋 Paste from Excel: add new projects, milestones or risks and issues by pasting rows from Excel (English or German column names). Every row is checked before anything is saved.
  • 📨 Change requests: Send to. Whoever submits a change request chooses who decides it: the sponsor, or the portfolio owner (for example when the sponsor is the one being changed). Portfolio owners see every pending request in their portfolios.
  • 🔄 One lifecycle: a project runs Not started → Running ⇄ On hold → Closed → Archived, and the form says who may do what.
  • ⚙️ Settings reorganised: six clear groups with a search box, and explanations behind ⓘ.
  • ⚡ Faster: history charts and Analytics read less data, and parts only Site Owners use load when opened. The app downloads less than before.

Also New & Improved

  • • Lessons learned on closed projects, also found by Search.
  • • Sortable tables: every column of the milestone and risk tables sorts both ways.
  • • Team allocation in finer steps: 1 %, 5 %, then 10–100 % in steps of 5.
  • • Date format setting: one fixed format for everybody, or each person's own SharePoint setting.
  • • Optional "Amber" instead of "Yellow" for the fourth rating, and a glossary ⓘ on the tiles.
  • • Baseline dates stay editable until the first status report or snapshot has recorded them.
  • • Overview screens refresh themselves when you come back after five minutes.
  • • Accessibility: reduced motion respected, clear focus rings in high-contrast mode, Esc closes every dropdown.
  • • A banner says when a list was too large to read completely, so totals are never silently short.

Security & Architecture

  • 🔒 Unchanged Security Footprint: no Microsoft Graph or API permissions, no calls outside the SharePoint site. The new "Request licence" button only opens your own mail program; the app sends nothing.
  • 🔒 No lost changes: saving never overwrites a change someone else saved in the meantime; you are asked to reload instead.

For Site Owners & Administrators

  • • Run Setup once after updating. Existing licence keys keep working.
  • • Settings is grouped into General, Modules, Projects, Status reports, Access & approvals, and Portfolios & exports, with search.
  • • Project types and phases: reorder, add or set inactive; rename or delete phases nothing uses yet.
  • • Licence: the trial shows a notice from day 20 and a banner from day 27, with a Request licence button that prepares an e-mail with your site's details.
  • • Health & Setup: copy diagnostics for support, and check change request decisions made outside the app.

Enterprise Maintenance & Updates

How updates are delivered, applied and tested in your tenant.

How do updates reach my tenant?

PPM Compass 360 is packaged as a standard Microsoft SharePoint Framework (SPFx) package (.sppkg). You upload the updated package to your tenant App Catalog and click Deploy. Zero server access or PowerShell scripting required.

How does data migration work during updates?

All your data lives securely in your own SharePoint Online lists inside your tenant. After each release, a Site Owner runs Setup once (the app shows a reminder); it adapts existing lists and items to the new version and keeps your historical status reports, milestones, risks, and audit logs.

Can I roll back to an older version?

No. Backward rollbacks are not supported. When a new release introduces structural changes to SharePoint lists (such as new columns, views, or calculations), the list schema moves forward. For this reason, we strongly suggest testing each new package on a staging/sandbox site before rolling it into production.

How do we get notified of new updates?

Customers receive proactive release notifications with download links and changelogs. You control the deployment timing in your tenant App Catalog and can validate on a sandbox site at your own pace.
