v1.7.4 | Cross-Project Capacity Intelligence, Locked Allocations & Adaptive Reporting

Latest Release
4 October 2026 — Production Build (SPFx 1.7.4.0 · Consolidates 1.7.x)

Package Version 1.7.4.0 (Consolidates 1.7.x)

No new lists or columns, and no Setup run needed when updating from 1.7.0 or later. Older versions run Setup once as before (see upgrade instructions).

Zero-Friction In-Place Updates: For updates that require no list schema modifications, Site Owners see a subtle update notice once per version rather than a setup prompt. The "run Setup" banner triggers only when a release introduces structural migrations.

What's New in Resource Intelligence & Adaptive Governance

  • ⚠️ Cross-Project Over-Booking Warnings: A compact ⚠ indicator appears directly inside the Resources rating card, project header, step 2 of status reports, and on My Portfolio whenever a team member is booked beyond capacity across all active projects over a rolling 3-month window. Updates dynamically without page reloads; projects on hold are automatically excluded.
  • 🔍 12-Month Cross-Project Booking Inspector: Click any team member's name or the ⚠ over-booking warning on the Team tab to inspect a complete 12-month cross-project allocation breakdown across every active project in the tenant, eliminating surprise resourcing collisions before work begins.
  • 🛡️ Automated Allocation Quality Checks: The Team tab actively validates resource health in real time: flagging team members booked past the project target end date, identifying upcoming milestones without allocated personnel, and highlighting extreme overallocation (>120%).
  • 🎯 Strategic Effort Tracking (FTE per Objective): Strategy view now displays planned human effort alongside financial budgets, showing planned team allocation as an average annual % FTE per objective to connect operational capacity directly to high-level strategic priorities.
  • 🔒 Finalized Baseline Allocations & Audit Locking: Submitting a monthly status report automatically finalizes and locks team allocations from two months prior (e.g. submitting the September report locks July, keeping August open for actual reconciliation). Finalized months display an audit lock badge (🔒) on the Team tab to prevent inadvertent edits.
  • 📈 Plan-vs-Actual Variance Tracking: Planned team values for future months are permanently recorded. Excel exports display both planned baseline and actual recorded effort, enabling automated variance reporting.
  • 📄 Adaptive Document Exports & True One-Pagers: Executive exports dynamically preserve clean layouts. When summary text overflows, font size scales adaptively before cleanly continuing to page 2; Goal/Scope/Success and Strategy links are intelligently included if room allows; drag-and-drop section reordering in the export dialog; and optional Strategy Flow slide in Executive Story exports.
💼 Business Impact & PMO Value

Cross-Project Resource Governance Without Expensive Add-ons: Enterprise PMOs gain portfolio-wide capacity intelligence without deploying expensive third-party resource management tools. Automated over-booking alerts prevent burn-out, 12-month inspector reveals cross-project commitments in one click, and baseline allocation locking (🔒) ensures historical capacity audits remain 100% trustworthy and tamper-proof.

For Site Owners & Administrators

  • • Zero-Touch In-Place Updates: When updating from 1.7.0+, no setup action is required. A one-time notice confirms the update without displaying unnecessary setup banners.
  • • Audited Month Reopening: The PPM team and portfolio owners can reopen a locked historical month on the Team tab ("Reopen a month…") with a mandatory justification reason, which is permanently logged in the project audit history.
  • • Streamlined People & Access Directory: User administration displays clean full names with compact action icons for role reassignments and removals, eliminating technical login strings.

Enterprise Maintenance & Updates

How updates are delivered, applied and tested in your tenant.

How do updates reach my tenant?

PPM Compass 360 is packaged as a standard Microsoft SharePoint Framework (SPFx) package (.sppkg). You upload the updated package to your tenant App Catalog and click Deploy. Zero server access or PowerShell scripting required.

How does data migration work during updates?

All your data lives securely in your own SharePoint Online lists inside your tenant. After each release, a Site Owner runs Setup once (the app shows a reminder); it adapts existing lists and items to the new version and keeps your historical status reports, milestones, risks, and audit logs.

Can I roll back to an older version?

No. Backward rollbacks are not supported. When a new release introduces structural changes to SharePoint lists (such as new columns, views, or calculations), the list schema moves forward. For this reason, we strongly suggest testing each new package on a staging/sandbox site before rolling it into production.

How do we get notified of new updates?

Customers receive proactive release notifications with download links and changelogs. You control the deployment timing in your tenant App Catalog and can validate on a sandbox site at your own pace.
