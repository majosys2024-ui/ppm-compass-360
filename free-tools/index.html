Business Case Builder

Turn an idea into a one-page proposal in about 15 minutes: problem, solution, strategic fit, benefits, cost, risks and the decision you need.

  • Guided 7-step flow with examples
  • Readiness score (0–100) shows what's missing
  • From a rough size to a year-by-year cash flow
  • NPV, IRR, payback and a downside/upside range
  • One-page PDF and .json to edit later

Project Health Check

Drop an export from Microsoft Planner, Jira or MS Project and get delivery metrics, milestone slip and a transparent 0–100 health score.

  • Planner, Jira, MS Project, Excel, CSV
  • Milestone slip against a baseline
  • PDF and PowerPoint export
  • Files are processed only in your browser
