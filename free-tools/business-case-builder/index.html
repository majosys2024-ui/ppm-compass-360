Analysis period

Suggestion: 8 %, a common hurdle rate for mid-sized companies. If your Finance team has a standard rate (often called WACC or hurdle rate), use theirs. Use a higher rate for riskier projects, or 0 % to show undiscounted figures.

Uncertainty range (optional)
Shows a downside (costs higher, benefits lower) and an upside (the reverse) next to your base case. Per-line ranges are available in the year-by-year cash flow.

Example: costs −10 % / +25 % and benefits −30 % / +10 %. Costs tend to overrun and benefits tend to come in lower, so ranges are usually lopsided.

Set the range for each line in the Range column of the cash-flow table above: how much lower and how much higher the amount could be, in % (for example −10 and +25).

    What makes a business case get approved

    The builder follows the questions steering committees ask, in the order they ask them. The readiness score checks that each one has an answer.

    1

    Problem before solution

    Committees fund problems they recognise. Quantify what doing nothing costs, and the solution sells itself.

    2

    Numbers you can defend

    From a rough size to a year-by-year cash flow with NPV, IRR and payback, plus a downside and upside range. Honest ranges beat false precision.

    3

    A clear ask

    The decision you need goes at the top of the one-pager: amount, people, time frame. No clear ask means no decision.

    Questions

    Is the Business Case Builder free?

    Yes. Every feature, including the PDF and .json downloads, works without signup or email.

    Is my business case uploaded anywhere?

    No. What you type stays in your browser. The draft is saved in your browser's local storage so you can come back to it, and the PDF is created on your device. Our privacy-friendly analytics (only if you accept cookies) mask the builder's content.

    How is the readiness score calculated?

    It is a 100-point checklist of what steering committees ask for: a clearly described problem (10), the cost of doing nothing (5), a solution summary (10), scope boundaries (5), alternatives (10), a strategic objective (10), quantified benefits (15), a cost estimate (10), at least two risks (5) with mitigations (5), resources and timeline (5) and a clear decision request (10). 80 or more is committee-ready; click any item in the checklist to jump to that step.

    How are NPV, IRR, payback and ROI calculated?

    Every cost mode produces yearly costs and benefits for Year 0 (set-up) to Year 3, 4 or 5. NPV discounts each year's net cash flow at your discount rate (8 % is suggested; use your Finance team's rate if they have one). IRR is the rate at which NPV is zero. Payback is the point where the cumulative cash flow turns positive. ROI is total benefits minus total costs, divided by total costs, over the analysis period. It is a planning model, not a full financial appraisal: no tax, inflation or depreciation.

    Can I show a downside and an upside?

    Yes. In step 5, set a percentage range for the whole business case (for example costs −10 % / +25 %, benefits −30 % / +10 %), or per line in the year-by-year cash flow. The downside uses higher costs and lower benefits, the upside the reverse. Tick “Show downside and upside” to include them in the preview and PDF.

    Can I give Finance the detailed numbers?

    Yes. Tick the financial appendix to add a second PDF page with the year-by-year table, key figures and a cumulative cash-flow chart, or download the cash flow as a .csv file for Excel.

    Can I edit my business case later or share it with a colleague?

    Your draft is restored automatically in the same browser. Click “Save .json” to keep a copy, move it to another device or send it to a colleague, then use “Open .json” (or drop the file on the builder) to continue.

    Can you build a calculator for our own model?

    Yes. If your organisation needs its own cost and benefit categories, a fixed hurdle rate, approval thresholds, additional figures such as TCO, a branded template, or a version inside your Microsoft 365, and we will come back with a proposal.

    How does this relate to PPM Compass 360?

    The builder helps you write one proposal. PPM Compass 360 runs the whole portfolio inside your Microsoft 365 tenant: intake, strategic alignment, milestones, budgets, risks and health reporting for every approved project.
