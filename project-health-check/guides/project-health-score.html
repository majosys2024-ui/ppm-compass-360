Project Health Score: how it is calculated

The Project Health Score sums up a plan in one number from 0 to 100. It is calculated in your browser from your export.

PartWeight100 means
Adherence to plan35%every task due in the last 30 days was done on time
Open tasks not overdue20%no open task is past its due date
Milestone slip20%open milestones are on their baseline (0 at an average slip of 60 days)
Tasks with due date and owner15%every open task has both
On-time completions10%every task finished in the last 30 days was on time

Parts without data (for example slip with only one export) are left out and the others are re-weighted. The tool shows each part and which one pulls the score down most.

Reading the score

  • 75–100 healthy
  • 50–74 needs attention
  • below 50 critical

The score is a conversation starter, not a verdict. Look at the next steps the tool suggests. Check your plan.
