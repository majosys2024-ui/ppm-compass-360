Free project health check for Microsoft Planner, Jira and MS Project

Load a task export and get delivery metrics, a Project Health Score from 0 to 100, a milestone timeline with slip against the baseline and concrete next steps for your steering meeting. No signup needed for one plan.

How it works

  1. Export your plan from Planner, Jira, MS Project, Asana, monday.com or Trello, or copy cells from any spreadsheet.
  2. Load the file above. Check the columns and confirm which tasks are milestones.
  3. Get the score, metrics, schedule check, next steps and the milestone timeline. Download PNG, CSV, a PDF report or a PowerPoint slide.

What you get

  • Adherence to plan (AtP), overdue work, due next 30 days, cycle time and workload by owner
  • Project Health Score with the part that pulls it down most
  • Milestone timeline with baseline vs forecast, phase band and today line
  • Schedule check with a next step for each finding
  • Portfolio view of up to 5 plans side by side (free with email)

Your project data stays in your browser

Files are read and analysed by your browser. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored on a server. The page only allows data to be sent through the optional email form, which contains your email and answers, never project data.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Project Health Check free?

Yes. One plan with two export dates works without signup. With a free email signup you can analyse up to 5 plans × 12 dates and get the portfolio view, the PDF report and a PowerPoint slide.

Which tools are supported?

Microsoft Planner (Excel export, English and German), Jira (CSV), MS Project (XML or Excel), Asana (CSV), monday.com (Excel), Trello (JSON) and any Excel or CSV table. You can also paste cells from a spreadsheet.

Is my project data uploaded?

No. Your files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded or stored. Only the optional email form sends data, and it contains no project data.

How is the Project Health Score calculated?

It combines adherence to plan (35%), open tasks not overdue (20%), milestone slip (20%), tasks with due date and owner (15%) and on-time completions (10%). See how the score is calculated.

How do I see milestone slip?

Load two exports of the same plan from different dates, or one MS Project XML file with a baseline. See milestone slip explained.

What is PPM Compass 360?

A portfolio app for Microsoft 365 that snapshots all your projects every month automatically, inside your own tenant, with full milestone trend analysis. This free tool covers one plan at the moments you upload.
