Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Project Health Check free?

Yes. Analyzing one plan with up to two snapshot dates is completely free without signup. With a free email unlock, you can compare up to 5 plans across 12 snapshot dates and access the portfolio view, PDF report, and PowerPoint slide.

Which tools and file formats are supported?

Microsoft Planner (Excel export in English and German), Jira (CSV export with all fields), MS Project (XML or Excel export), Asana (CSV), monday.com (Excel), Trello (JSON), and any standard Excel (.xlsx) or CSV table. You can also paste copied spreadsheet cells directly into the app.

Is any project data uploaded to your servers?

No. Your files are processed 100% in your local browser memory and are never uploaded, logged, or saved to any external server. Only the optional email form (used to unlock the 5-plan portfolio view) submits your contact answers, and it never sends any project data.

How is the Project Health Score calculated?

It uses a transparent, objective formula: Adherence to plan (35%), open tasks not overdue (20%), milestone slip (20%), tasks with valid due date and assigned owner (15%), and on-time completions (10%). See our detailed guide on how the score is calculated.

How do I see milestone slip?

Either load two exports of the same project plan taken at different dates (e.g. baseline and current), or load an MS Project XML export that contains an established baseline.

What is the difference between this tool and PPM Compass 360?

This free browser tool analyzes standalone task exports on demand. PPM Compass 360 is our full enterprise project portfolio management solution for Microsoft 365, which lives inside your SharePoint tenant and automatically takes monthly snapshots, tracks financial budgets, and steers enterprise portfolios.
