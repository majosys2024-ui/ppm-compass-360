Core Capabilities

Everything You Need to Run Projects & Portfolios

Six tightly integrated modules designed for real-world PMOs, eliminating the friction of multi-tool chaos.

📊

1. Portfolio Health & Calculated Overall RAG

Real-time calculated Overall RAG status automatically derived from the worst of 4 sub-RAGs: Timeline, Budget, Resources, Scope (mandatory commentary reason required for non-green ratings). Filter across customizable portfolios configured by the site owner.

  • • Calculated Overall RAG (worst-case rollup)
  • • Mandatory non-green narrative explanation
  • • Customizable portfolios & business units
🎯

2. Milestone Roadmaps & Timelines

Visual milestone tracking with baseline comparison and phase-gate governance. Clearly spot slips before they impact executive steering deliverables without forcing a micromanaging task planner.

  • • Key milestone schedules & phase gates
  • • Baseline vs. forecast variance
  • • Teams keep Jira, Planner, or Excel for daily tasks
⚠️

3. 3×3 Probability & Impact Risk Matrix

Interactive risk heatmaps mapped to probability and severity with 1-click risk-to-issue conversion. Log mitigations, assign risk owners, and filter by project or cross-portfolio exposure.

  • • Interactive 3×3 probability × impact matrix
  • • In-place risk-to-issue escalation
  • • Mitigation action owners & status
🧭

4. 5-Axis Health Radar & Modular Tabs

Optional 5-axis 360 Health Radar (Schedule, Cost, Output %, Team mood, Risk) toggleable from Admin Center. In early governance, Decisions and Team project tabs can be disabled for a leaner footprint.

  • • Admin switch: Health Radar on/off
  • • Modular tabs: Decisions & Team toggleable
  • • Captures Output % & team sentiment
💰

5. Financials & Excel Connector

Track planned budget vs. actual spend vs. EAC. Finance data is maintained in a dedicated admin list periodically populated via standard Excel copy/paste (automated Excel connector in active development).

  • • Strict separation of duties (PM cannot overwrite)
  • • Fast periodic update from finance Excel sheets
  • • Automated Excel connector in roadmap
🔄

6. Change Requests & Decisions Log

Formalize scope, cost, and timeline changes alongside an immutable Living Decisions Log that records who made what strategic decision, when, and why—killing hallway decisions forever.

  • • Sponsor approval routing (via Power Automate)
  • • Permanent, audit-locked Decisions Log
  • • Cost and schedule delta tracking
Reporting Philosophy

Make decisions from the live portfolio — not a PowerPoint slide.

Live Discussions Over Dead Slides

Traditional PMOs spend up to 40% of their working hours manually copy-pasting spreadsheet rows into static PowerPoint slides. PPM Compass 360 is built so governance reviews, gate approvals, and steering meetings happen live with the app on the screen.

Nevertheless, when executives or external partners need portable reports, 1-click generators create polished PowerPoint (.pptx) and PDF (.pdf) summaries instantly.

Structured Excel Copy: Total Peace of Mind & AI Analysis

The built-in Excel export (.xlsx) gives Project Managers a comprehensive, structured copy of all project data in one file.

This provides PMs with total peace of mind and data sovereignty: they hold all their records at any second, and can safely feed this clean tabular dataset into modern AI tools (Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini) for personal scenario planning and ad-hoc forecasting — without AI ever touching live SharePoint lists.

Enterprise Ready

Technical & Security Architecture

Release Version & Maintenance v1.0.3.x (Actively Maintained)

All updates automatically migrate existing list data forward to work with the new release without data loss, preserving all projects, historical status reports, and audit logs. Validating on a sandbox site prior to production upgrade is recommended.
Framework & Technology SharePoint Framework (SPFx) client-side web part (React / TypeScript).
Deployment Model Standard Tenant or Site Collection App Catalog upload (.sppkg). Ready in 5 minutes.
Capacity & Scale Testing Up to 200 projects per site (ideal for companies running up to 100 projects in parallel within a department). Rigorously tested and benchmarked with 230 projects and 10,000 risks and milestones; all future releases are validated against this baseline dataset.
Multi-Site Architecture For organizations running multiple business units (e.g., R&D vs. Manufacturing vs. Commercial), deploying separate SharePoint site collections is recommended to separate governance models, lists, and administration.
Governance Modularity & Toggles Admin Center switches allow toggling the Project Health Radar (5-axis spider charts, Output %, and Team mood) on/off. In early governance stages, the Decisions and Team project tabs can be turned off for a lean profile. Overall RAG is calculated automatically from the worst of the 4 sub-RAGs.
Finance Data & Connector Roadmap Maintained in an isolated admin list periodically populated via standard Excel copy/paste by the finance team (strict separation of duties). Automated Excel connector is in active development.
Workflow Automation Prerequisite Requires Power Automate enabled in the tenant to run approval workflows (Change Requests) and automated email reminders, configured separately on the respective SharePoint lists using standard M365 entitlements.
Data Storage Location 100% In-Tenant. Your portfolio data stays inside your Microsoft 365 / SharePoint environment. Automatically provisions native SharePoint Online lists within your site.
External Data Egress 0 KB. Zero external databases, zero tracking cookies, zero third-party telemetry.
Power Apps Dependency 0 € Required. Completely independent of Microsoft Power Apps and Dataverse premium licensing.
Security & Permission Model Zero "Members" requirement. Users stay in Visitors group; dynamic list permission breaking grants role-based write access. Lists are hidden from site navigation.
