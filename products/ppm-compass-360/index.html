Core Capabilities

Everything You Need to Run Projects & Portfolios

Six tightly integrated modules designed for real-world PMOs, eliminating the friction of multi-tool chaos.

📊

1. Portfolio Health & Governance

Real-time RAG (Red-Amber-Green) status indicators across scope, schedule, budget, and resources. Filter instantly by strategic portfolio, department, or stage-gate.

  • • Stage-gate status tracking
  • • Executive summary narratives
  • • Multi-criteria portfolio filtering
🎯

2. Milestone Roadmaps & Timelines

Visual milestone tracking with baseline comparison. Clearly spot slips before they impact executive governance gates and sponsor deliverables.

  • • Key milestone schedules
  • • Baseline vs. forecast variance
  • • Critical-path deliverable visibility
⚠️

3. 3×3 Probability & Impact Risk Matrix

Interactive risk heatmaps mapped to probability and severity. Log mitigations, assign risk owners, and filter by project-level or cross-portfolio exposure.

  • • Standard 3×3 matrix distribution
  • • Mitigation action owners & status
  • • Quantitative risk exposure tracking
👥

4. Resource Allocation & FTE Capacity

Monthly FTE heatmaps across your team. Highlight overallocated team members instantly and make staffing adjustments with clear utilization visibility.

  • • Monthly FTE heatmap view
  • • Over-allocation visual flags
  • • Role and discipline grouping
💰

5. Budget, Actuals & Variance

Track planned budget vs. actual spend vs. forecast at completion (EAC). Eliminate financial surprise with real-time portfolio burn-rate analysis.

  • • CAPEX and OPEX categorization
  • • Spend variance calculation
  • • Rolling financial forecast

6. Change Request Audit Trail

Formalize scope, cost, and timeline changes. Every change request is logged with sponsor sign-off status, financial delta, and operational impact description.

  • • Sponsor approval routing
  • • Cost and schedule delta audit
  • • Historical change log
Reporting Philosophy

Screen-First Governance — With Built-in Excel, PowerPoint & PDF

Live Discussions Over Dead Slides

Traditional PMOs spend up to 40% of their working hours manually copy-pasting spreadsheet rows into static PowerPoint slides. PPM Compass 360 is built so governance reviews, gate approvals, and steering meetings happen live with the app on the screen.

Nevertheless, when executives or external partners need portable reports, 1-click generators create polished PowerPoint (.pptx) and PDF (.pdf) summaries instantly.

Structured Excel Copy: Total Peace of Mind & AI Analysis

The built-in Excel export (.xlsx) gives Project Managers a comprehensive, structured copy of all project data in one file.

This provides PMs with total peace of mind and data sovereignty: they hold all their records at any second, and can safely feed this clean tabular dataset into modern AI tools (Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini) for personal scenario planning and ad-hoc forecasting — without AI ever touching live SharePoint lists.
