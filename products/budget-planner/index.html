Most project tools only track tasks or flat budgets. Budget Planner structures your project financials across Idea, Planning, Execution, and Closing with proven estimating disciplines.

🛡️ PMBOK Reserves

Say goodbye to arbitrary 10% buffers

Sponsors and CFOs reject arbitrary contingency percentages. Budget Planner computes mathematically defensible reserves based on your project's quantified risk register and estimating uncertainty.

  • ✓ Expected Monetary Value (EMV): Quantified probability × impact for identified risk events (the "known unknowns").
  • ✓ Estimating uncertainty spread: Statistically aggregates (P80 − P50) across individual item confidence ranges.
  • ✓ Documented basis of contingency: Project managers must record the written rationale for baseline commitment, making budgets fully auditable.
  • ✓ Management Reserve (%): Sponsor-held reserve for unforeseen changes, separate from PM contingency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the early adopter program.

What does "Free for Early Adopters" mean?

Organizations that deploy Budget Planner during our early adopter window receive full access without license fees. We only ask that you share constructive feedback, report any edge cases, and help us validate features against your real-world budgeting requirements.

How is Budget Planner installed?

Budget Planner is a standard SharePoint Framework package (.sppkg). Your Microsoft 365 administrator uploads it to your tenant App Catalog and adds it to any target SharePoint site. Setup takes under two minutes with automated list provisioning.

Does Budget Planner access or transmit external data?

No. Budget Planner runs 100% clientside in your browser and stores financial data exclusively in SharePoint lists within your own tenant boundary. It makes zero network calls to external databases, APIs, or analytics servers.

Can team members work without SharePoint delete rights?

Yes. Budget Planner features a strict "no delete" architecture. Removing an item marks it as obsolete and archives its history, meaning site members can operate safely with restricted permissions without needing delete rights.
