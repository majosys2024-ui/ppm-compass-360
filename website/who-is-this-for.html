The PPM Architecture Your IT Cannot Say "No" To

Why did we omit built-in alerting servers and proprietary AI integrations? Because keeping the core app light means zero security friction for IT, while giving you clean, structured Microsoft 365 lists that power your favorite tools.

⚡

Native Power Automate

No proprietary alerting code. Set up turnkey workflows directly on your SharePoint lists:

  • • Change Request Approvals: Route Teams/Outlook approval cards to the Sponsor.
  • • Monthly Report Alerts: Automatically notify leadership when a monthly status is locked.
Uses your existing M365 licenses with zero add-ons.
🤖

Safe Copilot & AI Agents

Want to use AI? Download the full project snapshot Excel file (.xlsx) and feed it directly into Copilot or ChatGPT:

  • • Original Data Untouched: AI has zero write access to your SharePoint records.
  • • Structured AI Analysis: Clean 4-way RAG and baseline drift data gives AI the clarity needed for reliable summaries and Copilot Studio agents.
Zero hallucinations modifying your portfolio data.
📊

Power BI for Complex Reports

When executive management demands custom, multi-tiered corporate dashboards:

  • • Direct SharePoint Connector: Power BI connects natively to PPM lists without data gateways.
  • • ERP & Financial Blending: Merge PPM data with SAP, Oracle, or CRM systems seamlessly.
Zero expensive PPM BI add-on subscriptions.
🔒

Zero "Member" Access Architecture — Everyone Stays in the Visitors Group

Your IT team will love this: no regular user ever requires broad SharePoint "Members" or "Contribute" permissions to the site collection. The built-in Admin Settings page breaks list permission inheritance to grant write access to Visitors dynamically based on their specific role in each project or portfolio. It also lets administrators hide the underlying SharePoint lists from navigation and Site Contents so users cannot bypass the UI or tamper with records directly.
