Everything You Need to Run Your PMO

4-Way RAG Health Reporting

Track Red, Amber, and Green status across four distinct dimensions: Timeline, Budget, Resources, and Scope, alongside an overall project health score and mandatory RAG commentary.

Phase Gates & Baseline Drift

Enforce governance milestones and stage-gate approvals. Track baseline dates against actual forecast dates to immediately identify schedule slip, with one-click snapshot capture in PM_APP_MilestoneSnapshots.

3×3 Risk & Issue Matrix

Standardized 3×3 Likelihood × Impact scoring matrix (Low, Medium, High). Filter risks visually by cell density and worst severity, and convert materializing risks into active issues with a single click, preserving audit trails in PM_APP_RisksIssues.

Financials Separation of Duties

Resource Allocation Heatmap

Visual monthly FTE percentage tracking for Developers, PMs, Architects, and QA. Spot over-allocated team members immediately across multiple concurrent projects (PM_APP_ProjectAllocations).

Locked Monthly Status Audits

Create executive monthly progress summaries with key achievements and next steps. Once published, reports are cryptographically marked "Locked against further edits" to ensure regulatory and PMO audit compliance.

Dedicated "My Portfolio" Cockpit

Dedicated Portfolio Manager workspace with 5 interactive KPI drill-down tiles (RAG Health, On-Time Delivery, Financial Burn with data gap transparency, Active Blockers, and Phase Distribution), secondary compliance tracking, and scoped portfolio roster.

Direct Share Links & 6-Color Milestones

Zero External Data Egress & Complete Project Snapshots

No data goes out — everything stays 100% in your SharePoint tenant. All computations occur entirely in the user's browser. Project Leaders (PL) can export the entire project snapshot as an Excel file (.xlsx) for deep ad-hoc analysis, in addition to one-click executive 1-pager PowerPoint (.pptx) and PDF (.pdf) export options.

🔒
Zero "Member" Access Architecture — Everyone Stays in the Visitors Group

Your site data is dramatically safer. Users do not need broad SharePoint "Member" or "Contribute" permissions to the site collection. The built-in Admin Settings page breaks list permission inheritance on PPM lists to grant write access to Visitors dynamically based on their assigned role in each project or portfolio. It also lets administrators hide underlying SharePoint lists from navigation and Site Contents so users cannot bypass the app UI or tamper with records directly.

Quick Start

Deploy in 3 Simple Steps

From download to live portfolio in under five minutes.

Step 1

Upload .sppkg

Upload ppm-compass-360.sppkg to your SharePoint Tenant App Catalog (apps.sharepoint.com). Check "Enable this app and add to all sites".

Step 2

Add Web Part

Open your modern SharePoint PMO site. Edit the page, click the + tool, and select "PPM Compass 360".

Step 3

Workspace Setup

Click the built-in "Workspace Setup" button. The app provisions and indexes all necessary SharePoint lists automatically. You're ready to manage!
