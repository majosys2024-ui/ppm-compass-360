Pricing & Licensing Questions

How does a "Per Site Collection" license work?

A license key is tied cryptographically to your SharePoint Site Collection ID. Any user who has access to that SharePoint PMO site (whether 5 people or 500 people) can view and edit projects, milestones, and risks without incurring any additional fees.

How do we enter our license key?

Once purchased, you will receive your cryptographic license key. Inside the app, click the About / License dialog in the top navigation, paste your key, and click Save. The license is automatically stored in your local PM_CFG_AppSettings list and takes effect immediately.

How does the Multi-Site Tenant License work?

Our Multi-Site Tenant License is priced at exactly 3× the annual 1-year price (11.970 € / year). It allows you to deploy and activate the app across unlimited site collections within your Microsoft 365 tenant (e.g. IT PMO, Engineering, Finance, Operations, Corporate Strategy). If you run 4 or more PMO sites, this tier immediately saves you money.

Does any project data or telemetry leave our Microsoft 365 tenant?

No data goes out — everything stays 100% inside SharePoint. The app is a client-side SharePoint Framework (SPFx) web part running strictly in the user's browser. It communicates directly and exclusively with standard SharePoint lists in your own tenant via HTTPS. Project Leaders (PL) can also export the entire project snapshot as an Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx), or export executive 1-pager PowerPoint (.pptx) and PDF (.pdf) summaries directly from the project workspace.

What is the 3-Year Limited-Time Offer?

The 3-Year Limited-Time Offer grants early adopters a 50% discount off our standard list price, locking in 2.490 € / site / year (7.470 € total for 3 years, saving 7.500 € compared to the 4.990 € list price). This price lock protects your organization from inflation and rate increases for a full 36-month period.

Can we set up approval flows, email notifications, or connect Power BI and Microsoft Copilot?

Yes—without paying for extra SaaS add-ons. The app is purposefully engineered with zero custom notification servers or external API dependencies. Because your data is stored in standard SharePoint lists, you can:

  • Power Automate: Create native approval flows for Change Requests or automated email alerts to Sponsors when monthly status reports are published.
  • Power BI: Connect directly to PPM SharePoint lists via native M365 connectors to build custom corporate dashboards and blend with ERP/SAP data.
  • Microsoft Copilot: Project Leaders can export clean Excel snapshots (.xlsx) and feed them to Copilot or custom Copilot Studio agents. Your live SharePoint data remains 100% untouched and safe from AI write operations, while AI receives structured data for consistent variance analysis.

The solution package (.sppkg) is sent directly via email upon request. You can upload it to your SharePoint App Catalog for a full 30-day evaluation trial with unlimited projects and portfolios. For commercial licensing and organizational terms, simply contact us directly.
