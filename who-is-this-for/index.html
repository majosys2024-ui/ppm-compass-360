Structured Excel Copy & Safe AI

The built-in Excel export (.xlsx) gives Project Managers a comprehensive, structured copy of all project and portfolio data in one file:

  • • Total Peace of Mind: PMs own every data point at any second — milestones, risks, finances, RAG history — with zero platform lock-in.
  • • AI-Ready Data Structure: Feed the clean tabular export directly into Copilot, ChatGPT, or Claude for personalized forecasts, scenario planning, and executive briefings.
  • • Zero Data Egress Risk: Safe, read-only extraction means AI never modifies underlying tenant SharePoint records.
Full data sovereignty + safe AI exploration.

Power BI for Complex Reports

When executive management demands custom, multi-tiered corporate dashboards:

  • • Direct SharePoint Connector: Power BI connects natively to PPM lists without data gateways.
  • • ERP & Financial Blending: Merge PPM data with SAP, Oracle, or CRM systems seamlessly.
Zero expensive PPM BI add-on subscriptions.
🔒

Zero "Member" Access Architecture — Everyone Stays in the Visitors Group

Your IT team will love this: no regular user ever requires broad SharePoint "Members" or "Contribute" permissions to the site collection. The built-in Admin Settings page breaks list permission inheritance to grant write access to Visitors dynamically based on their specific role in each project or portfolio. It also lets administrators hide the underlying SharePoint lists from navigation and Site Contents so users cannot bypass the UI or tamper with records directly.
