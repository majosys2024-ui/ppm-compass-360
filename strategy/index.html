Strategic Governance & Auditing

Eliminate Unfunded Goals and Rogue Project Spend

Traditional PPM tools force executives to dig through dozens of status decks to find out if strategy is actually being executed. The Strategy view immediately highlights two critical governance risks:

⚠️

Unfunded Strategic Objectives

Corporate objectives that have been committed at the board level, but have zero active projects or budget allocated to deliver them.

Executive Benefit: Catch unfulfilled board promises early during annual portfolio intake rather than during year-end governance reviews.
🔍

Unlinked / Rogue Project Spend

Active projects consuming staff hours, contractor budget, and PMO bandwidth that are not linked to any corporate objective or initiative.

Executive Benefit: Stop pet projects and unauthorized capital drain by requiring project sponsors to justify strategic fit before approval.
Project-Level Alignment

Strategic Fit Directly on the Project Goal Card

Strategy is not an isolated slide hidden in a corporate portal. In PPM Compass 360, every project overview features an explicit Strategy: line in its core goal card.

  • ✔ Up to 3 Strategic Links: Projects can support multiple strategic initiatives, but must designate exactly one primary link.
  • ✔ Curated by Project Leaders & Sponsors: The Project Leader, Deputy, and Sponsor edit links with the inline pencil icon.
  • ✔ Objective Owner Verification: Strategic objective owners review linked projects and can remove connections that do not genuinely fit.
  • ✔ Clean Budget Totals: In configuration, choose whether budget counts only under the primary link (preventing double-counting) or across every link.

Enterprise Governance Standards

Non-Destructive Lifecycle & Total Data Control

Strategy management is designed to comply with strict enterprise IT and audit standards:

📁
Non-Destructive Retirement

Strategic objectives and initiatives are never permanently deleted. When an objective concludes or priorities shift, items are cleanly Retired, preserving full historical audit logs for prior status reports.

🛡️
Site Owner Governance

The strategy module is an optional setting toggleable by Site Owners. Only authorized governance leads can create, reorder, or retire strategic nodes in the Configuration center.

🔒
100% In-Tenant Sovereignty

All strategic nodes, linkages, and assessments live strictly inside your own enterprise cloud boundary. Zero third-party databases, zero external telemetry, zero vendor lock-in.
