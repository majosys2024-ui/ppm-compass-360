## Welcome to the Partner Enablement Kit

This directory contains the complete operational, commercial, and technical toolkit designed to help consulting practices bundle PPM Compass 360 into high-margin advisory engagements.

When your clients are stuck in spreadsheet chaos, this toolkit enables you to deliver structured PMO governance on the Microsoft 365 SharePoint Online they already own—with zero per-seat software taxes and 5-minute IT approval.
