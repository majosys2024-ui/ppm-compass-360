The Problem You Face Today

Why "First PMO Governance" Engagements Stall

You spend weeks designing beautiful project charters, stage-gate governance frameworks, and RAG definitions. But when it comes to technology, you hit one of three walls:

1

The Excel & PowerPoint Rut

Clients default to shared Excel workbooks and slide decks. Within 3 months, formulas break, version control collapses, and the steering committee is back to arguing over outdated figures.

Result: Your governance methodology dies with the spreadsheet.
2

Heavy Enterprise PPM Overkill

Recommending Planview, Clarity, or Jira Align triggers a 9-month procurement nightmare, $50,000+ software contracts, and user rebellion from project managers who just wanted a clean status overview.

Result: Budget consumed by software licenses, not your advisory fees.

The Power Apps License Trap

Building on Power Platform requires $20/user/month Power Apps licenses plus Dataverse storage fees. For 100 stakeholders, that is $24,000/year in Microsoft tax before you even start configuring.

Result: Client CFO rejects the recurring per-seat software quote.
