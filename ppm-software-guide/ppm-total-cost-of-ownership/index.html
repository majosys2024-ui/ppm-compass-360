PPM Software Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Guide

A 3-year financial model for CFOs, CIOs, and PMO Leaders comparing the true lifecycle cost of Enterprise SaaS PPM, Power Apps (Dataverse), and Flat-Fee SharePoint SPFx.

⏱️ 9 min read · Updated October 2026 · Part of the PPM Software Guide Hub →

🧊 1. The PPM Software Cost Iceberg

When evaluating PPM software, most procurement teams only compare the advertised sticker price (e.g. "$35 per user per month"). However, in enterprise software, direct subscription licensing represents less than 40% of the true 3-year cost.

What Lies Beneath the Surface of a PPM Budget:
1. Implementation Consulting

Third-party system integrator fees to configure forms, workflows, and database tables. Typically ranges from 15.000 € to over 80.000 €.

2. The "Viewer Tax" Inflation

Cost escalations as line managers, risk owners, and steering committees require accounts just to view monthly RAG summaries.

3. Database & Storage Overages

Tiered storage costs (such as Microsoft Dataverse database/file capacity at $40/GB/month) as years of attachments and milestones accumulate.

4. Internal Maintenance FTE

Ongoing internal IT overhead needed to maintain custom connectors, schema changes, and ALM deployment pipelines.

📉 3. Why Per-Seat Pricing Models Damage PMO Adoption

The financial cost of per-user licensing is obvious, but the organizational friction is often worse. When an organization must justify a $20 to $45/user/month subscription fee for every user, PMO managers are forced to ration accounts.

1. Shadow Spreadsheets

Team leads without licenses revert to tracking risks, budgets, and milestones in local Excel sheets, completely fragmenting portfolio transparency.

2. Account Sharing Hazards

To bypass licensing costs, teams share generic logins, destroying audit logs, change traceability, and Entra ID compliance.

3. Delayed Approvals

Stakeholders cannot easily review change requests or sign off on stage gates inside the tool, forcing manual email chains and slide decks.

A flat-fee per-site license (as used in PPM Compass 360) solves this structurally: because adding 500 stakeholders costs exactly the same as adding 5, PMOs can grant read-only visibility to the entire enterprise without budget anxiety.

🔓 Zero Exit Friction: Eliminating Migration Lock-In & Expensive SQL Extraction

Most enterprise TCO calculations fail to budget for the hidden cost of leaving. Legacy PPM suites and proprietary Dataverse solutions often demand $15,000 to $30,000 in database engineering fees or complex SQL ETL scripts to extract historical project milestones, budgets, and audit logs when graduating to new systems.

Because PPM Compass 360 stores all records directly in standard, open SharePoint Online lists inside your tenant, your data is never held hostage in a proprietary database prison. You can export complete project and portfolio snapshots to Excel (.xlsx) with one click, or query the lists directly via Microsoft Graph API and OData—ensuring 100% data portability whenever your organization is ready to scale to the next level.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the PPM Compass 360 annual license?

The license covers unlimited users, unlimited projects, all core modules (Registers, 5-Axis Health Radar, Capacity Heatmap, Risks, Milestones, Decisions, Change Requests), the Strategy alignment module, regular SPFx package updates, and enterprise email support.

Are there any hidden Microsoft cloud storage fees?

No. All lists, views, and documents are stored directly inside your existing SharePoint Online tenant. A typical 200-project portfolio consumes less than 50 MB of list data, utilizing standard SharePoint storage you already pay for with Microsoft 365.

Can we test the software before committing financial budget?

Yes. We provide a full 30-day evaluation package deployed directly into your own SharePoint Online sandbox or staging site, complete with sample data and full setup guidance.

What happens if our organization wants to migrate or scale to another tool?

You face zero vendor lock-in and zero data extraction fees. Because all records are stored in standard SharePoint Online lists inside your tenant, you can export full portfolio snapshots to Excel (.xlsx) with one click, or query the lists directly via Microsoft Graph API and OData. There is no proprietary database to reverse-engineer and no expensive SQL migration consulting required.
