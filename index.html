PPM Practitioner Principle

Make decisions from the live portfolio - not a PowerPoint slide.

.xlsx .pptx .pdf
Discussions happen on the screen, not in disconnected decks. Our goal is that all portfolio reviews, status discussions, gate approvals, and steering decisions happen directly with the live app on the screen - eliminating the hours PMOs lose manually compiling slides and re-formatting tables.
Complete data peace of mind + AI-ready tabular structure. The built-in Excel export provides a structured, complete copy of your entire portfolio and project dataset in one clean format. PMs own all their data at any second and can directly feed it into modern AI models (Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini) for their own custom analysis, ad-hoc forecasting, and deep insights.
