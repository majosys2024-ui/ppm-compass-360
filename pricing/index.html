Frequently Asked Questions

How does the "Per Site Collection" license work?

A license key is tied directly to your SharePoint Site Collection ID. Any user who has access to that SharePoint site (whether 10 team members or 500 stakeholders) can view and manage projects, milestones, risks, capacity, and executive reports without incurring any per-user licensing fees.

How does the Multi-Site Tenant License work?

The Multi-Site Tenant License is priced at 14.000 € / year and includes our dedicated Implementation & Technical Onboarding Package (up to 8 hours). It allows your organization to activate PPM Compass across unlimited site collections within your Microsoft 365 tenant (e.g. IT PMO, Engineering, Operations, Transformation, Corporate Strategy). Each department operates with full data isolation and independent governance, while our team assists your M365 administrators with App Catalog deployment, topology planning, and initial training for PMO leads, site administrators, and portfolio owners.

Prefer a longer-term commitment? What multi-year options are available?

If your organization prefers multi-year budget predictability and fixed procurement cycles, talk to us about multi-year options. We provide multi-year agreement structures tailored for enterprise procurement processes.

Does PPM Compass replace Microsoft Planner, Jira, or Azure DevOps?

No — and that is deliberate. PPM Compass sits at the macro portfolio governance layer. Delivery teams can continue using whatever day-to-day task tools suit their workflow best (Microsoft Planner, Jira, Azure DevOps, or simple checklists). PPM Compass gives leadership the structured, single source of truth for portfolio steering, milestones, capacity, and decisions without forcing a disruptive task-tool migration.

Does any project data or telemetry leave our Microsoft 365 tenant?

No data goes out — everything stays 100% inside your Microsoft 365 tenant. The app is a client-side SharePoint Framework (SPFx) web part running in the user’s browser. It communicates directly and exclusively with standard SharePoint lists in your own tenant via HTTPS. Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), and PDF (.pdf) exports are generated locally in the browser; they are never sent to external servers.

Are all modules and capabilities included in the license?

Yes. Every license includes all 11 core capabilities: Portfolio Management, Strategy & Objectives, Project Health, Milestones & Phase Gates, Risks & Issues, Capacity & Resources, Financials, Change Management, Decisions, Executive Reporting, and Exports & Automation. There are no module add-ons or locked features.

Are all listed prices inclusive or exclusive of VAT?

All listed prices are exclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT) and regional sales taxes. Taxes are applied on your corporate invoice according to your organization's legal registration and billing country. For EU business customers with a valid VAT ID, the standard EU B2B reverse charge mechanism applies.

What scale of projects and company size does PPM Compass support?

PPM Compass is engineered for organizations with 50 to 1,000+ employees. Each licensed site collection easily handles up to 200 concurrent projects and has been benchmarked with over 10,000 milestones and risks. For multi-department environments (e.g. IT, Operations, R&D), the Multi-Site Tenant license allows deploying isolated hubs across the organization.

How is the software delivered and evaluated?

The solution package (.sppkg) is provided for deployment directly to your SharePoint App Catalog. Organizations can request a full 30-day evaluation package to test the complete software inside their own Microsoft 365 environment with their team.

How are product updates and data migrations handled?

PPM Compass is actively developed with continuous enhancements. Updating involves deploying the new package to your App Catalog; our automated in-app setup checks preserve all existing project data, historical status reports, decision records, and audit logs. See our Release Notes for changelogs.
